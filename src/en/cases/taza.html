About the project

Taza is a marketplace for wholesale purchasing. Shops and cafés order goods directly from distributors and manufacturers, while suppliers manage orders, product range and analytics there. Less manual work for both sides

Challenge

  • Design a convenient app for ordering goods directly from distributors — without phone calls and Excel files
  • Create a supplier dashboard for uploading the product range, managing orders and tracking payments
  • Design UX flows for fast product search and filtering that account for wholesale specifics (units of measure, packaging, price lists)
  • Make the process transparent for everyone involved: order status, delivery, documents
  • Create a responsive design that accounts for how people use different devices (in the office, at the warehouse, on the go)
