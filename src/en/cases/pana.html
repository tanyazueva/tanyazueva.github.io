About the project

Pana is an aggregator of short-term rentals and events with a powerful ecosystem for B2C and B2B users. Hotels, apartments, tours, workshops and other activities are all in one service. Client: Pana Boutique Hotels, a large boutique hotel chain in Kazakhstan

01

Searching for and booking stays and experiences

  • Browse offers as a list or on a map
  • Filter by features and price
  • An “Experiences” section with detailed event descriptions
  • Pay by card or in installments

02

Reviews and interaction

  • Text and audio reviews of stays and events
  • Chat with the property owner or event organizer
  • Built-in auto-translation for talking with foreign guests

03

Corporate clients and extended options

  • Flexible hotel room rates depending on the services included
  • Business trip booking and corporate accounts
  • A waitlist for popular rooms
  • Ordering one-time or recurring cleaning

04

B2B dashboard for property owners and experience organizers

We built a convenient dashboard with analytics and booking management

Dashboard features

  • Dashboard with analytics for each property
  • Owner registration and verification by documents or video
  • Integration with TravelLine — the largest hotel platform
  • Adding properties, bulk adding rooms for hotels
  • Manual booking management and early check-out with recalculation
  • Connecting cleaning services and gyms
  • Chat with guests, replies to reviews, reputation management
  • Staff access control and roles
  • Financial analytics tools and report downloads

05

Smart pricing

One of the key features is automatic market price analysis that takes into account:

  • Seasonality (for example, high demand in resort areas)
  • Major city events (concerts, conferences, marathons)
  • Historical data from previous years

The system recommends optimal prices 14 days ahead, and the owner can apply them in one click or adjust them manually

06

Web version and moderation panel

  • The web version fully mirrors the mobile app’s functionality
  • Admins can moderate reviews, support requests, disputes and property photos
  • Built-in profanity filter

07

Illustrations and graphics

To support the brand style, I created 30 illustrations adapted for the interface. They’re used in modals, empty states and marketing materials

🖌 Process: hand sketches → vectorization → refinement in Adobe Photoshop

Takeaways

Pana is a large-scale project with 1,000+ frames and dozens of complex features. Working on it, I:

  • Got deep into the hospitality business and how hotels operate
  • Built a scalable design system that’s used in the company’s new products
  • Sharpened my skills in managing design processes and working with development
  • Learned to delegate, manage large tasks and work effectively in a team
